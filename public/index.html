THE BRIEFING

How to play

Read the past. Outsmart the present. Guess all three dials to win instantly.

01 · Take one action

On your turn, reveal a clue for +2 points, privately verify a clue for −1, secretly interfere with one dial for −2, or play an Event Card for +1. Actions move the hidden engine.

02 · Lock your prediction

Once every player has acted, secretly choose a value from 0–3 for A, B and C. Your guess stays hidden until everyone locks.

03 · Read the result

One correct dial earns +1. Two earn +3. A perfect guess wins immediately. Otherwise, a hidden automatic event changes the machine before the next round.

THE CLUES ARE REAL

Every clue records a true dial value from an earlier version. Interference and Event Cards change the machine, so old facts can go stale.
